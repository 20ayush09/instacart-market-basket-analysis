# Instacart Customer & Sales Analysis

> **Power BI Analytics Report | Customer Behavior • Retention • Product Affinity • Churn Risk**



## Executive Overview

This project analyzes Instacart customer purchasing behavior to identify the behavioral patterns that influence **customer retention, product affinity, purchase frequency, and churn risk**.

Using a point-in-time snapshot of **3M+ orders, 206K customers, and 34M products sold**, the analysis examines reorder behavior, category concentration, purchase timing, customer purchase velocity, and basket sequence dynamics.

The objective is not only to describe what happened, but to translate behavioral patterns into **commercial opportunities around customer lifetime value (CLV), retention, cross-selling, and promotional targeting**.

---

## Business Context

E-commerce grocery platforms operate under persistent pressure from customer acquisition costs, category concentration, and customer churn.

Understanding how customers reorder, which categories drive sales, when customers purchase, and how purchasing behavior changes throughout the customer lifecycle can help identify opportunities to:

* Strengthen customer retention
* Increase customer lifetime value
* Diversify product purchases
* Identify declining purchase velocity
* Improve promotional timing
* Develop targeted customer interventions

This analysis uses Instacart transaction data to investigate these areas and translate the results into actionable business recommendations.

---

## Objectives

The analysis focuses on five core business questions:

1. **Reorder & Loyalty**
   What percentage of orders are repeat purchases, and how does reorder behavior relate to customer engagement?

2. **Category Concentration**
   Which product categories contribute most to sales, and where does diversification opportunity exist?

3. **Purchase Timing**
   When do customers place orders, and what day-of-week or time-of-day patterns emerge?

4. **Churn Risk**
   Which customer behaviors indicate declining purchase frequency or potential churn?

5. **Basket Dynamics**
   How does product position within a basket relate to purchase volume and reorder behavior?
